Power BI — Sustainability Dashboard

12-month UAE office energy dataset. 
4 visuals: monthly energy, carbon emissions, 
energy vs target KPI, total CO2e card.

Key finding: July-August account for 20.0% of 
annual emissions — HVAC driven.

DEWA 2025 grid emission factor: 0.3833 kg CO2e/kWh
EGBC benchmark: 160 kWh/m2/year

The dashboard visualises 12 months of UAE office energy data across 4 charts: monthly energy consumption, monthly carbon emissions, energy vs target KPI, and total CO₂e card. Data is benchmarked against Emirates Green Building Council standards (160 kWh/m²/year) and the DEWA 2025 grid emission factor (0.3833 kg CO₂e/kWh).
